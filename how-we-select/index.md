## An independent guide, not an official ranking

SkillNorth is an independent editorial project. We organise course options into skill clusters to help learners understand where a course may fit. A SkillNorth shortlist is not an official Alison ranking, endorsement or guarantee of career outcomes.

## What we check

1. **Topic fit.** The course should sit clearly within the cluster and support a recognisable learning goal.
2. **Course-page evidence.** We review the public title, description, stated learning outcomes and any level or duration displayed by Alison.
3. **Practical usefulness.** We favour topics that can be connected to a practice task, project or work situation.
4. **Realistic scope.** We distinguish introductory learning from professional licensing, regulated qualifications and advanced technical competence.
5. **Maintenance.** Course titles, availability, languages, duration and syllabi can change. We mark review dates and recommend checking the live Alison page before enrolling.

## How to read our course cards

A level or duration is shown only when it was visible in the Alison page reviewed. Where it was not clear, the card says to check Alison's current course page. That is intentional: we do not fill gaps with guesses.

## Why the clusters differ from Alison's categories

Alison groups its catalogue into broad categories such as Information Technology, Health, Business, Management, Personal Development, Sales & Marketing, Engineering & Construction and Teaching & Academics. SkillNorth combines or narrows those categories around learner questions. For example, the IT category is separated into AI, data, cybersecurity and programming so that each page can answer a more specific intent.

## What a course can—and cannot—do

A course can provide structured learning and a way to practise a topic. It cannot guarantee a job, substitute for professional experience or automatically meet local licensing requirements. For regulated areas such as healthcare, teaching, finance and engineering, verify the requirements that apply in your location.

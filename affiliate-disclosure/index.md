## Our relationship with Alison

Some links on SkillNorth are affiliate links to Alison. If you follow an affiliate link and register or take an eligible action, SkillNorth may receive compensation from the partner. This does not increase the price you pay where the partner programme terms provide that arrangement; check Alison's current terms for details. The link does not change the editorial criteria we use to select or describe courses.

We aim to explain why a course appears in a cluster, what it covers and what its limits are. We do not present affiliate placement as an official Alison ranking, and we do not guarantee employment, salary or certification outcomes.

## How to recognise an affiliate link

Buttons such as “Explore Alison courses” and “Find this course on Alison” use the Alison partner link configured by the site owner. In this version, the shared link opens Alison rather than a uniquely tracked page for each individual course. We display an affiliate note near key calls to action and in the site footer.

## Course costs and certificates

Alison states that learners can enrol in, study and complete its courses for free. Digital or printed certificates and diplomas may be offered as optional purchases after completion. A free Learner Record is available. Always check the current course page and Alison's terms for the latest details.

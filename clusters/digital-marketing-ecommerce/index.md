## What to learn in this cluster

Digital marketing is not simply posting on social media. It involves understanding an audience, choosing a message, selecting channels and measuring whether the activity achieved its purpose. A funnel can help connect awareness, consideration and action, while email and landing pages support the journey beyond the first interaction.

Start with the digital-marketing overview, then examine the AI-focused course as a second step. Treat AI as an assistant for research, ideation or analysis—not as a substitute for brand judgment, factual review or consent-based marketing.

## Who this is for

This hub is useful for small-business owners, aspiring marketers, content creators and professionals who need to understand how digital channels support a business goal.

## Skills to practise beyond the course

Digital marketing is a sequence of decisions: understand an audience, set an objective, shape an offer, choose a channel and measure the result. Learn how search, social content, email, paid media and landing pages play different roles. A campaign should have a clear audience and a relevant metric; impressions alone do not show whether the activity created meaningful interest or action.

E-commerce adds product pages, pricing, checkout, customer service, fulfilment and post-purchase experience. Marketing and commerce therefore depend on trust, accurate product information and a smooth customer journey. AI tools can support brainstorming or analysis, but every claim, image, recommendation and automated message still needs human review and appropriate consent.

## A practical way to apply what you learn

Create a mini campaign brief for one product or service. Specify the audience, need, offer, message, channel and one primary success measure. Draft a landing-page outline and a short follow-up email, then identify the privacy and platform rules that apply. This creates a coherent practice project without needing to spend money on ads or collect personal data.

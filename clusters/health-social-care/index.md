## What to learn in this cluster

Health and social care learning covers people, settings and responsibilities—not just facts about conditions. Foundational topics include person-centred support, consent, safeguarding, communication, ethical practice and infection prevention.

Use the selected diploma to explore the subject and understand its vocabulary. Before treating any course as career preparation, check the formal training, supervised practice and professional registration rules in the country where you intend to work.

## Who this is for

This hub is a starting point for learners interested in care settings, existing support workers reviewing foundational knowledge, and people exploring whether the field matches their interests.

## Skills to practise beyond the course

Health and social care is centred on people, dignity and safety. Foundational topics include person-centred support, consent, safeguarding, confidentiality, communication, infection prevention and recognising when a concern must be escalated. The right response depends on the setting, the person's needs and the policies that govern the service. Good care also involves listening and respecting an individual's preferences rather than assuming that one approach fits everyone.

This hub can help you explore terminology and decide which area interests you, such as support work, community care or further study in health-related subjects. It is not a substitute for supervised clinical practice, statutory training, professional registration or local safeguarding requirements. Before pursuing a role, check the official regulator and employer requirements in the country where you intend to work.

## A practical way to apply what you learn

Use a fictional case study to identify the person's stated preferences, potential risks, consent considerations and the appropriate person to contact for help. Explain how you would protect confidentiality and document a concern according to a given policy. Do not practise clinical procedures on others or use a general online course as authority to perform regulated tasks.

## A more thoughtful way to choose what to learn

SkillNorth is an independent learning and career-skills guide for an international audience. We believe a strong learning decision starts with a real capability you want to build—not simply a job title that appears popular today.

We organise learning resources into focused clusters, explain what a course covers and suggest ways to turn study into practice. Our goal is to make the next step easier to understand, not to promise a particular career outcome.

## Our editorial principles

- **Practicality:** explain what learners can do with the knowledge.
- **Clarity:** distinguish introductory courses from formal qualifications.
- **Transparency:** disclose affiliate relationships and editorial selection criteria.
- **Adaptability:** revisit course information because catalogues and skills evolve.

SkillNorth is not Alison's official website. Alison is the learning platform we reference in selected course recommendations.

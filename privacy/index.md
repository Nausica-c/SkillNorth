## Draft notice — review before launch

This page is a starter privacy notice and is not legal advice. Before making the site public, the site owner must review it for the countries served, confirm the hosting and contact details, and update it to match any tools or services actually used.

## Site operation

SkillNorth is designed as a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. This repository does not add analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or newsletter forms by default. The hosting provider may process technical data needed to deliver and secure the site; consult GitHub's current privacy documentation for details.

## External links

Some pages link to Alison through an affiliate URL. When you follow an external link, you leave SkillNorth and the destination provider's privacy and cookie practices apply. Review Alison's own notices before creating an account.

## Cookies and analytics

No optional analytics or marketing scripts are included in this starter build. If they are added later, this notice and any required consent mechanism must be updated before deployment.

## Contact and data requests

A contact email has not yet been configured. Add the site owner's public contact address in `_config.yml` and update this section before launch.

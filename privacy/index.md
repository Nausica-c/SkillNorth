## About this notice

This notice describes the current SkillNorth website as configured in this repository. SkillNorth is an independent editorial project and is not Alison's official website. This page is general website information, not legal advice. The public contact address for SkillNorth is skillnorthsite@gmail.com. The publisher/operator should also publish the relevant legal identity and review the notice for the markets in which the site operates.

## Information processed when you visit

SkillNorth is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. The site does not currently provide visitor accounts, comment forms, contact forms, newsletter registration or user-upload features. It does not include Google Analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or third-party font services in this build.

GitHub states that when someone visits a GitHub Pages site, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub may therefore process technical request information as part of delivering and protecting the site. SkillNorth does not have direct access to GitHub's hosting logs. For details about GitHub's processing, consult the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and the [GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages).

## Cookies, analytics and local storage

The current SkillNorth code does not add analytics or advertising cookies and does not use local storage to identify visitors. GitHub's own infrastructure and any browser-level functionality are governed by their respective providers' terms and privacy information. If analytics, advertising, consent tools, embedded content or other tracking technologies are added in the future, this notice must be updated and any legally required consent mechanism must be implemented before those tools are activated.

## External links and affiliate links

Some links lead to Alison. Some of these are affiliate links. When you select an external link, your browser connects to the destination provider and that provider may receive technical information, set cookies or use other technologies under its own privacy notice and terms. SkillNorth does not control Alison's data practices. Review [Alison's website](https://alison.com/) and its current privacy information before creating an account or enrolling in a course.

## Information you choose to send

The current site does not contain a form that collects personal information. If you email skillnorthsite@gmail.com, SkillNorth will receive the email address and any information you choose to include in the message. The mailbox is intended for editorial questions, corrections and collaboration enquiries. Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, health information or other sensitive personal information by ordinary email. Messages should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and any appropriate follow-up. The mailbox is provided by Google; Google may process message and account data under its own terms and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data requests and contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to SkillNorth, contact [skillnorthsite@gmail.com](mailto:skillnorthsite@gmail.com). For information about data processed directly by GitHub, use the contact and privacy channels described in GitHub's own privacy statement. The identity and jurisdiction of the publisher/operator should be added to this notice where required by applicable law.
